How to pay

1. Send the money

Send the amount with bKash, Nagad or Rocket (Send Money) to the agent's number shown in the Wallet page.

2. Submit the request

In the app open Wallet → Add credits, pick your plan, enter your sender number and the Transaction ID (TrxID), then submit.

3. Get credited

An admin verifies your payment, the credits are added to your balance instantly and you can see the full history in the Wallet page.

Good to know

What credits are spent on

Chat with premium models, video generation, image generation and research. Each request shows its cost before it runs. Economy mode uses cheaper models automatically.

Free tier

You get a free daily token allowance for the built-in models — no payment needed. Bring your own API key and chat without spending credits at all.

Credits expiry

Purchased credits are valid for 30 days from the day they are approved. Free daily allowance resets every day.

Support

Payment taking too long to approve? Contact the admin from inside the app. All approvals and history are recorded in your Wallet.
